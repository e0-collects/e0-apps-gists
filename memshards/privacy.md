# Privacy Policy for Memshards

**Effective date:** October 1 2026

Memshards ("the game") is published by Ezero Collective ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

## Summary

- You do not need an account, and we do not collect or receive any personal information about you.
- Your game progress stays on your device.
- The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to deliver those ads.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only:

- your level progress, star ratings, scores and remaining chances
- your Daily Challenge and Endless results
- your play calendar, daily goal and settings (music, sound, vibration, difficulty)

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not included in device backups. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in your device settings deletes it permanently.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one to refill your chances. They are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID
- your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
- device information, such as model, operating system version and language
- how you interact with ads, and diagnostic and performance data

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. More detail on how Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

We do not receive this information, and we do not sell personal information.

### Your choices about ads

- **European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland:** when you first open the game you are asked whether you consent to the use of your data for advertising. You can change your choice at any time in the game under **Settings > Ad privacy choices**.
- **All regions:** in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads) you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation.
- Watching ads is always optional. If you never watch an ad, your chances still refill over time.

## Fonts

The game requests its typeface through Google Play services on your device. This is handled by Google under the privacy policy linked above.

## Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Because we do not collect or store personal information, we hold nothing to provide or delete. Game data is on your device and under your control. For information collected by Google for advertising, use the controls described above or Google's tools at <https://myaccount.google.com>.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
